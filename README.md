I made this under Hack Club 's jumpstart program , in which we ship sick games and get to start a haven in our city. It is a parkour game . Your name is mask guy , you belong to another who travelled to earth by mistake through a mystric portal . Your family is waiting for you in your world . You have to do parkour in order to reach to portal and go to your place . 

Controls :

press "a" to move left

press "d" to move right

press space  bar for jump

In main menu click "start" to start the game.
